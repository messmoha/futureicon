Privacy Policy – FutureIcon Publisher

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. Who we are

FutureIcon Publisher ("the App") is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the FutureIcon AI channels to post our own videos to TikTok accounts we own.

2. Data we access

When an account owner authorizes the App, TikTok provides:

  • basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) – used only to confirm which of our accounts is connected;
  • an access token and refresh token – used only to upload and publish our own videos to that account.

We do not access followers, messages, viewing history or any other TikTok user's data.

3. How data is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on our private server, are never shared or sold, and are deleted when access is revoked.

4. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any data to third parties.

5. Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. After revocation we delete the stored tokens.
